WHO WE ARE

Creative work, made personal.

TimoTECH Films is a photography and videography studio dedicated to telling powerful stories through the lens. From weddings and events to music videos, commercial shoots, documentaries, and social content, we bring creativity and care to every production.

01

Our approach

We combine creative vision, cinematic techniques, and careful editing to make visuals that feel genuine and stand the test of time.

02

Our experience

We work with individuals, brands, and organizations to create quality visual stories, with a focus on collaboration and attentive service.

03

Rooted in Tharaka Nithi

Based in Kaare Market, Mwimbi Ward, Maara Subcounty, Tharaka Nithi County, Kenya. Our mobile team can travel to nearby regions and beyond for shoots.

THE PERSON BEHIND THE LENS

Meet the founder

TimoTECH Films was founded by Timothy Mugambi, a passionate photographer and videographer with over 15 years of experience. His dedication to the craft and eye for detail shape the studio's personal approach to visual storytelling.
